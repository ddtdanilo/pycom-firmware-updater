# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Everyone should be able to contribute to keeping existing hardware usable without
harassment or discrimination. Participate respectfully regardless of experience,
identity, nationality, disability, or technical background.

## Expected behavior

- Be patient, constructive, and specific when giving or receiving feedback.
- Critique ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Respect privacy, attribution, copyright, and hardware ownership.
- Accept correction and acknowledge uncertainty or mistakes.
- Keep discussion focused on the project and help newcomers understand its scope.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexualized conduct, deliberate
disruption, impersonation, and sharing someone's private information without
permission are unacceptable. These expectations apply in project issues, PRs,
reviews, and other project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Contact the maintainer privately through
[the maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/ddtdanilo) using an available
private contact method. If no private contact is listed, open an issue requesting
a private conduct-reporting channel without identifying the affected people or
publishing sensitive details. Security reports use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

There is no independent project conduct contact yet. If a report concerns the
maintainer, GitHub's [platform abuse reporting](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse)
is available for conduct that violates its policies. List an independent project
contact here if a co-maintainer accepts that role; do not imply one exists today.

The maintainer will treat reports discreetly and may warn, remove content, restrict
participation, or ban a participant depending on severity and repetition. Actions
should be proportionate, explained where privacy permits, and open to reconsideration
when new evidence is available. Maintainers are subject to the same conduct rules.
